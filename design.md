# S-ARC — SOUTHERN ARCHITECTURE

## Homepage UI/UX Design

### 1. Project Overview

Design a premium, minimalist and sophisticated homepage for **S-ARC (Southern Architecture)**, a multi-vendor architectural marketplace connecting architects, designers, homeowners, contractors and property developers.

The platform allows customers to discover, explore and purchase architectural designs based on their land dimensions, preferences, budgets and building requirements.

The website should combine the visual sophistication of a luxury architectural magazine with the usability of a modern digital marketplace.

**Core design philosophy: Less decoration, more architecture.**

"Spaces Worth Living In."

**E. Explore Architectural Styles**

### 4. Interaction & User Experience

Design subtle and sophisticated interactions:

* Smooth navigation transitions.
* Elegant hover effects.
* Image zoom on architectural cards.
* Interactive search dropdowns.
* Refined button feedback.
* Smooth scrolling.
* Subtle section reveal animations.
* Responsive mobile navigation.

Prioritize usability over decorative animations.

### 5. Visual Design Rules

IMPORTANT:

* Avoid generic e-commerce layouts.
* Avoid generic SaaS dashboard aesthetics.
* Avoid excessive gradients.
* Avoid glassmorphism.
* Avoid oversized rounded cards.
* Avoid excessive shadows.
* Avoid unnecessary decorative elements.
* Avoid overcrowded layouts.
* Avoid bright, distracting colours.

Instead:

* Use generous whitespace.
* Use large architectural imagery.
* Maintain precise alignment.
* Establish strong visual hierarchy.
* Use understated borders.
